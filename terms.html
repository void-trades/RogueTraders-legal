TikTok Notifier: Terms of Service Last updated: 23 November 2026 TikTok Notifier ("the app") is a personal tool run by Rogue Trader for one purpose: posting a notification in the Rogue Trader telegram channel when Rogue Trader uploads a new video or goes live. Who can use it. The app is only used with the TikTok account of its owner. It isn't offered to other users, and no one else can sign in to it. What it does. With the owner's permission, through TikTok's official Login Kit, the app reads the owner's basic profile details and list of recent videos. When a new video appears, or the account goes live, it posts a short message and link to Telegram. What it doesn't do. The app doesn't post, edit or delete anything on TikTok, doesn't access messages, and doesn't act on behalf of anyone else. No warranty. The app is provided as is. Notifications can be late or missed, for example if TikTok's services change or are unavailable. Stopping. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions), and the app will stop. TikTok's terms.
