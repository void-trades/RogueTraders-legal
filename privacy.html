Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what the RogueTraders TikTok notifier ("the app") does with data. The app is a private tool used only by its owner, for their own TikTok account.

What the app reads

  • From TikTok, with the owner's permission (scopes user.info.basic and video.list): the owner's basic profile (display name and ID) and the owner's public videos (ID, title, cover image, link and posting time).
  • Whether the owner's account is currently live, from TikTok's public live page.

It does not read anyone else's data, messages, followers, or private content.

How it is used

Only to notice when the owner posts a new video or goes live, and to post an announcement (title, cover image and link) to the owner's Telegram channel. The data is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to build profiles.

Where it is kept

On the owner's own computer: the TikTok sign-in tokens, and the IDs of videos already announced (so nothing is announced twice). Nothing is stored on a server. Announcements posted to Telegram are subject to Telegram's privacy policy.

How long it is kept, and removing it

Until the owner deletes it. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, after which the app can no longer read anything.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
