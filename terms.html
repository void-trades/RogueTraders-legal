What the app does

When the owner's TikTok account publishes a new video or starts a live stream, the app posts a short announcement with a link to it in the owner's Telegram channel. It reads the owner's own TikTok profile and public video list through TikTok's official API, with the owner's permission. It does not post, edit or delete anything on TikTok.
