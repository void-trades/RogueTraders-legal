TikTok Notifier: Privacy Policy Last updated: 23 November 2026 TikTok Notifier ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner, Rogue Trader, with their own TikTok account. What data the app accesses Basic profile information: display name and account ID (TikTok scope user.info.basic). The list of the owner's own public videos, including video ID, title, description, upload time, cover image and share link (TikTok scope video.list). Whether the owner's account is live. How it's used. Only to detect new videos and live streams, and to post a short notification with the video's title, cover image and link to the owner's Telegram channel. What's stored. The app stores the login tokens TikTok issues and the IDs of videos it has already announced, so nothing is announced twice. These are kept locally on the owner's computer. No other data is stored, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties, apart from the notification posted to Telegram. Other people's data. The app doesn't access or store data about any other TikTok user. Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the app's local files removes everything it stored. Contact. therealroguetrader@proton.me
